CV-Saathi

Make your CV by talking

Answer a few questions in English, ಕನ್ನಡ or हिंदी. In about 10 minutes you get a one-page English CV as a PDF.

What we record

  • Your voice goes to Google's Gemini AI to understand you. The recording is not saved.
  • Gemini processes your voice on Google servers in the USA, and writes your draft CV on Google servers that may be in other countries outside India.
  • We save the written conversation and your CV details until you close or start over, and never more than 7 days.
  • We never ask for caste, religion, Aadhaar, marital status or date of birth.
  • Nothing is shared unless you download or share it yourself.
